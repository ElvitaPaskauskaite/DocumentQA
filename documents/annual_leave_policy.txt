Acme Ltd is a fictional company. This Annual Leave Policy was written to test the DocumentQA project and does not describe a real organisation.

The Acme Ltd Annual Leave Policy is owned by the HR department. Version 3.0 took effect on 1 January 2026 and applies to all employees on Acme Ltd employment contracts. Contractors are not covered by the Annual Leave Policy.

Full-time employees receive 23 days of annual leave per leave year. Irish public holidays are given in addition to annual leave.

Employees receive one extra day of annual leave for every 5 completed years of service, up to a maximum of 26 days of annual leave per leave year.

Part-time employees receive annual leave pro rata. For example, an employee who works 3 days a week receives 3/5 of the full-time entitlement, which is 13.8 days.

The annual leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

Up to 5 unused days of annual leave may be carried over into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March, or they are lost.

Annual leave is requested through the HR portal. Requests for more than 3 consecutive days of annual leave must be made at least 2 weeks in advance.

Taking more than 10 consecutive working days of annual leave needs approval from a director, in addition to the line manager.

Employees in the Finance team cannot take annual leave during the first 5 working days of each quarter, because of the quarterly financial close.

If an employee is sick during annual leave and provides a medical certificate, the sick days are not counted as annual leave and are returned to the employee's leave balance.

When an employee leaves Acme Ltd, any unused annual leave is paid in the final salary payment.

Questions about the Annual Leave Policy should be sent to hr@acme.example.
