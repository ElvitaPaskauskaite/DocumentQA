Acme Ltd is a fictional company. This Procurement Policy was written to test the DocumentQA project and does not describe a real organisation.

The Acme Ltd Procurement Policy is owned by the Procurement team within Finance. Version 1.4 took effect on 1 March 2026 and applies to all purchases of goods and services made on behalf of Acme Ltd.

Purchases under €1,000 need one quote and approval from the employee's line manager.

Purchases of €1,000 or more but under €10,000 need three written quotes from different suppliers and approval from the head of the department.

Purchases of €10,000 or more must go through a formal tender process and need approval from both the Finance Director and the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

A purchase order (PO) must be raised and approved before anything is ordered from a supplier. Acme Ltd does not pay invoices that do not include a valid purchase order number.

Splitting one purchase into several smaller orders to stay below an approval threshold is not allowed and is treated as a breach of the Procurement Policy.

New suppliers must be added to the approved supplier list before any order is placed. Adding a new supplier takes 5 working days and requires a valid tax clearance certificate.

All software and SaaS (software as a service) purchases must be reviewed by the IT Security team before they are bought, whatever the amount.

Acme Ltd pays supplier invoices within 30 days of the invoice date.

Employees may accept a gift from a supplier only if its value is €50 or less. Every supplier gift must be recorded in the gifts register, whatever its value.

A company credit card can only be used for single purchases under €500. Each company credit card has a monthly spending limit of €2,000.

Questions about the Procurement Policy should be sent to procurement@acme.example.
